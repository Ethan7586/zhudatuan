# Referral commission

- Trigger: the `referral` job consumes immutable order events from `runtime.inbox`; a settlement payload processes commissions whose `eligible_at` has passed.
- Owner: operations owns member review; finance owns rates, settlement exceptions, and money repair.
- Idempotency: the event handler locks `runtime.inbox` with consumer `job:referral`, verifies the matching `runtime.outbox` row, performs all writes, and sets `processed_at` in one transaction. A replay of a processed event is a no-op.
- Ordering: paid, received, cancelled, and refunded events must observe the committed `order.placed` referral inbox row. An out-of-order job rolls back and retries instead of completing a no-op before commissions exist.
- Evidence: event payload values are locators only. Scope, order state, member, line amount, tender allocation, and successful refunds are read from their authoritative tables before any commission change.
- Calculation: all amounts are integer minor units. Benefit-funded value is removed proportionally from every line with downward rounding; each commission uses `base_minor * rate_bps / 10000` with downward rounding.
- Depth: an order may create one direct commission and one immediate inviter reward. No higher relationship is queried.
- Settlement: `pending` moves to `settling` only for its snapshotted `settle_trigger`; the delayed job posts the remaining amount to `referral.commission.payable.<member>`, stores its journal on the commission, and marks it `settled`. Before the member can withdraw, the same transaction applies that new payable oldest-first to any recovery receivable, posts `referral.commission.recovery.offset` (debit payable, credit receivable), and appends one immutable offset movement per recovery source.
- Cancellation and refunds: rows are never deleted. Pending amounts retain their row and accumulate `reversed_minor`. A settled reversal posts a balanced `referral.commission.reversed` journal and appends a `reversal` movement. Partial refunds are recomputed cumulatively to avoid rounding drift. Submitted, approved, or failed referral withdrawals touching the commission are cancelled first; processing or uncertain payouts block the reversal until finance resolves them.
- Paid-after-refund recovery: if already consumed commission exceeds the post-refund entitlement, the difference posts as `referral.commission.recovery.accrued` (debit member receivable, credit member payable) and appends an immutable accrual movement linked to the reversal. Consumed means paid withdrawal claims plus prior recovery offsets funded by that commission. Outstanding recovery is `sum(accrual) - sum(offset)`; withdrawable commission is `amount - reversed - held claims - offsets funded by that commission`. Future settlements clear this balance automatically without editing paid claims or historical journals.
- Concurrency: reversal, settlement, and withdrawal take the same member row lock first, then commission rows in deterministic order. An immutable `order.cancelled` or `payment.refunded` outbox fact whose referral inbox is absent or unprocessed is also a fail-closed payout gate: new withdrawal creation and normal transitions into `approved` or `processing` fail with `REFERRAL_WITHDRAW_REVERSAL_PENDING`, including the outbox-to-inbox relay window. The only exception is recovery of an `uncertain` referral payout, which moves directly to `processing` so the idempotent provider request can reach `paid` or `failed`; it never re-enters the ordinary approval queue. The reversal then retries and either cancels the failed claim or accrues recovery for the paid amount.
- Stop loss: pause the `referral` job kind if scope evidence differs, commission bases no longer match order facts, or ledger posting fails. Do not mark the inbox row processed and do not edit commission rows manually.
- Diagnosis: inspect the outbox/inbox pair, order lines, benefit tender, successful refund tenders, binding expiry, active referral members, product rate snapshot, commission row, movements, and journals in that order.
- Recovery: correct the authoritative source, resolve an in-flight payout to a terminal state, then requeue the same event envelope. For a settlement failure, requeue the same scope; existing settled rows are skipped and deterministic journal/movement references make retries idempotent.
- Cutover: on a non-empty environment, backfill and replay `order.placed` outbox/inbox facts for all in-flight orders before registering or enabling the `job:referral` consumer. Do not bypass the ordering gate: later paid, cancelled, or refunded facts intentionally retry or dead-letter until their committed placed fact exists.
- Validation: no commission without a valid binding; at most two beneficiaries; benefit value excluded; only the configured trigger advances state; movement totals equal `journal_id` postings; `reversed_minor <= amount_minor`; processed event replay creates no rows. A referral withdrawal cannot enter `approved` or `processing` unless its immutable claims are non-empty, match scope/member/currency/source, remain in the prior withdrawal state, and sum exactly to the withdrawal amount.
- Escalation: finance controller for posted-money differences, operations lead for binding/member issues, and engineering on-call for evidence or scope mismatches.
