# Channel degradation

## Trigger and impact

Trigger on provider health degradation, circuit open, 429/timeout spike, statement mismatch or sync lag. Channel owner leads with provider owner.

## Stop loss and diagnosis

Disable/canary only the affected connection. Hide or reject uncertain checkout capability; never substitute mock products. Inspect signed manifest, secret version, health, rate/bulkhead/circuit and operations.

## Recovery and data repair

Rotate/fix connection, half-open a bounded probe, then resume saved cursor and reconcile statements/external objects.

## Validation and escalation

Contract version/manifest signature match, health is stable, source counts/hashes reconcile and rollback remains available. Escalate to vendor by contract SLO.

## Audit and postmortem

Record connection transitions, probes, cursor, errors, affected scopes and supplier communication.
